§Privacy notice

How Arch3 handles personal data

Effective 9 November 2026 · Written for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

1. Who we are

Arch3 makes a 3D sales explorer that real-estate developers put on their websites. You can reach us at privacy@arch3.in.

2. Two roles, depending on where you meet us

On arch3.in we decide what is collected, so we are the Data Fiduciary for it.

On a developer's website that uses Arch3, the developer decides what happens to enquiries and is the Data Fiduciary. Arch3 processes that data only on the developer's behalf and on their instructions, as a Data Processor. Questions about an enquiry you made on a developer's site are best sent to that developer; we will help them answer.

3. What we collect on arch3.in

  • Nothing by default. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ad trackers or third-party fonts.
  • If you book a call, the calendar is provided by Cal.com and loads only when you open it. Cal.com receives the name, email and any notes you enter, to schedule the call. Their privacy policy applies to that booking.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, to reply to you.

4. What the 3D explorer collects on developers' websites

  • Browsing the 3D building needs no personal data. The explorer reads public availability (available, on hold, booked, sold) and nothing about you.
  • An enquiry asks only for what a sales callback needs: your name, phone number and the flat you're interested in, and it asks for your consent first, saying what the details are for.
  • Developers' sales staff sign in to update availability. We keep their sign-in email for that purpose.

5. Why, and on what basis

We use personal data only for the purpose it was given for: replying to you, scheduling a call, or passing an enquiry to the developer you asked. The basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as easily as you gave it, by writing to privacy@arch3.in. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything done before it.

6. How long we keep it

Only as long as the purpose needs. Enquiry data is kept for as long as the developer instructs and is deleted when they ask or when the purpose is served. Emails and booking details are deleted once they're no longer needed for the conversation.

7. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) everywhere, and strict browser security headers.
  • Staff sign-in, and database rules that let only signed-in staff change availability.
  • No secret keys in the browser; access is limited to the people who need it.

If a personal data breach happens, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and the people affected, as the DPDP Act requires.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask for a summary of your personal data and how it's used, ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it, withdraw consent, nominate someone to exercise these rights for you, and have a grievance answered. Write to privacy@arch3.in. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

Arch3 is meant for adults buying or selling homes and is not directed at children.

11. Changes

If we change this notice we'll update the date above, and tell you directly where the change affects data you've already given us.
