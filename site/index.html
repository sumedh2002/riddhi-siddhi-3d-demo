06Security & privacy

Built for the DPDP Act, 2023.

Buyer data belongs to the builder. Arch3 collects only what a sales callback needs, asks first, and keeps it locked down.

  • Consent first

    Enquiries ask for clear consent and say what the details are for, before anything is sent.

  • Only what's needed

    Name, phone and the flat. No ad trackers, no third-party analytics and no cookies on this site.

  • Locked-down access

    Staff sign-in, and database rules that only let signed-in staff change availability. Buyers can only read it.

  • Encrypted

    HTTPS everywhere, strict security headers and no secrets in the browser.

  • Your buyers' rights

    Access, correction and deletion on request, handled for the builder within the law's timelines.

  • A named contact

    A grievance officer you and your buyers can write to. Read the privacy notice.

07FAQ

Questions builders ask.

What do you need from us?

Your brochure's floor plans and elevations are enough to start. CAD or architectural drawings make it even more exact, and we'll ask for them if you have them.

How long until it's live?

A few days for Basic and Pro on a typical project. Premium takes longer because every view is made for your project.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Most buyers will open it on a phone, so it's designed for touch first, with pinch, drag and full screen.

Where does it go on our website?

Anywhere you can paste a line of code: your project page, a landing page for ads, or a QR code at the site office.

What happens to enquiries?

They go straight to your team or your CRM with the flat, floor and source attached. Arch3 handles them only on your behalf.

Can it match our brand?

Yes. Colours, fonts and light or dark mode are set per project, so it looks like part of your site.
